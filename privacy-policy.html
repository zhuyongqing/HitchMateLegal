Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

In short: No account. No subscription. Your rotation, pay and certificate data stay on your device unless you choose to share them. You can turn anonymous usage data off at any time in Settings.

1. Information you enter

HitchMate stores the schedule and pay details you type in, so it can show you your calendar:

  • Rotation setup: name, days on and off, anchor date, shift type, travel rule and work time zone.
  • Day overrides and notes you add to individual dates.
  • Pay profile: currency, day rate, per diem, overtime multiplier and overtime hours.
  • Credentials: name, issuing body, issue and expiry dates and notes.
  • Preferences: home time zone, week start, holiday display and reminder settings.

This information is written to the app's private storage on your device. It is not sent to us or to any server we operate.

2. What we do not collect

  • No account, sign-in, email address or phone number.
  • No contacts, no calendar, and no location data.
  • No advertising identifier and no cross-app tracking.
  • No rotation, pay or certificate content on any server.

3. Anonymous usage data

This version of HitchMate does not send usage data anywhere. The Settings switch at Settings, Privacy, "Share anonymous usage data" is there so that anonymous diagnostics can be turned off before they are ever introduced. If we add them, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the switch will control it.

4. How your data is used

Your data is used on your device, for you: to draw the calendar and year view, count down to your next home day or hitch, mark holidays, estimate pay, and schedule local reminder notifications.

5. Notifications

Hitch and certificate reminders are local notifications created by iOS on your device. Notification permission is optional; if you decline it, the rest of the app still works. We do not operate a push server.

6. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with third parties. Data leaves your device only when you make it leave, for example by exporting a PDF, CSV, ICS or JSON file, or by using the iOS share sheet. What happens to a file after you share it is outside our control.

7. Widgets

The home and lock screen widgets read your current rotation from a private App Group container that is shared between the app and its widget extension on your device. This share stays inside your device; nothing is transmitted to us.

8. Backups and export

You can export a JSON backup of your data and restore it on another device yourself. Export and import are entirely under your control. iCloud sync is not part of this version.

9. Retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. To erase everything, open Settings, Danger Zone, "Delete all data". Removing the app from your device also removes its stored data.

10. Children

HitchMate is a tool for working adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
